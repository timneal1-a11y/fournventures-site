Our company

About fourNventures

A family real estate partnership built to own, care for and hold property over the long term.

Who we are

fourNventures, Ltd., also known as 4N Ventures, is a family-owned real estate company. It was organized as a Texas limited partnership in 2007 and is managed by its general partner, fourNventures Management, LLC. The partnership is owned by members of one family.

Our history

The partnership was formed in 2007 to hold the family’s real estate investments under one company. Over time it has built a portfolio centered on student housing close to two Texas universities: Texas Christian University (TCU) in Fort Worth and Baylor University in Waco. It also holds commercial property in Fort Worth and an interest in a commercial building in Springville, Alabama.

In 2025 the family reorganized its holdings so that its property-owning companies are held through fourNventures, Ltd..

Our approach

  • Long-term ownership. We buy properties we intend to keep, and we invest in maintaining them.
  • Location close to campus. Our residential properties are within the neighborhoods around TCU and Baylor, where students want to live.
  • Local professionals. Day-to-day leasing, repairs and maintenance are handled with local property-management and maintenance professionals.
  • Straightforward communication. Owners, partners, residents and neighbors can reach the company directly by email.
