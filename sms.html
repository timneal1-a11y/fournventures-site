Text message program

4N Partner Text Updates

Operational text messages from fourNventures, Ltd. to its partners who choose to receive them.

About the program

Program name: 4N Partner Text Updates (“4N Assistant”)
Sender: fourNventures, Ltd. (4N Ventures), from the toll-free number (877) 265-2010
Who receives messages: only the owners/partners of fourNventures, Ltd. who opt in. Residents, customers and the public are not enrolled, phone numbers are never bought, rented or imported, and no marketing or promotional messages are sent.
Message types: account and operational notifications about the company’s properties and partnership business, such as document-ready notices, reminders, payment and deadline alerts, and replies to questions the partner texts in.
Frequency: message frequency varies (typically a few messages per month).

How to opt in

Consent is optional and is never a condition of any purchase, service, or partnership interest. A partner can opt in either way:

  1. Written consent form. The partner fills in the form below with their name, title and mobile number, ticks the consent checkbox themselves (the box is blank and not pre-checked), signs and dates it, and returns it to tim.n@fournventures.com.
  2. Text keyword. The partner texts START to (877) 265-2010 from their own mobile phone.

Consent statement (on the form): “I agree to receive operational text messages (updates, reminders and alerts) from fourNventures, Ltd. at the mobile number I provide. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message frequency varies; msg & data rates may apply; reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

By ticking the box and signing, you agree to receive recurring operational text messages from fourNventures, Ltd. at the mobile number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Read our Terms of Service & SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

After you opt in

You receive this confirmation: “4N Assistant: You're subscribed to partner updates from fourNventures, Ltd. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.”

Example message: “4N Assistant: Reminder - the partnership documents are ready for your review in Teams. Reply STOP to opt out.”

Help and opting out

Reply STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, END, QUIT, CANCEL) at any time to stop messages; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or email tim.n@fournventures.com. Message and data rates may apply. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy.
