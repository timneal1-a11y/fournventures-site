Leasing & inquiries

How to reach us about renting a home, a current lease, or a business matter.

Prospective residents

Our student homes near TCU (Fort Worth) and Baylor (Waco) are leased for the academic year, with leases typically starting in August. Availability changes each year. To ask about upcoming availability, email tim.n@fournventures.com with:

  • the area you are interested in (near TCU or near Baylor),
  • the number of bedrooms and residents,
  • your preferred lease start date, and
  • the best way to reach you.

We will reply, or connect you with the property manager for that home, within one to two business days.

Current residents

For maintenance requests, rent payments and lease questions, please use the contact details provided with your lease or by your property manager. If you cannot reach them, email tim.n@fournventures.com. For emergencies involving fire, gas, or danger to life, call 911 first.

Commercial and business inquiries

Brokers, lenders, vendors, neighbors and other businesses can reach the company at tim.n@fournventures.com, or by mail at fourNventures, Ltd., 6235 Retta Mansfield Rd, Burleson, TX 76028.

fourNventures, Ltd. does not take rental applications, deposits or payments by text message or through this website.
