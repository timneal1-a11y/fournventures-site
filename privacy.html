Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how fourNventures, Ltd. (“4N Ventures,” “we,” “us”), a family-owned Texas real estate company, collects, uses and protects personal information, including information related to our text message program, 4N Partner Text Updates, from (877) 265-2010 or (877) 798-5444.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, mailing address and phone number (for example, when you email us about leasing or business matters).
  • Leasing and property information needed to lease and manage our properties, provided by residents, applicants and our property managers.
  • Text messaging information: for partners who opt in to our text program, the mobile number, the date and method of opt-in, and the messages exchanged.

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, has no account login, and does not collect personal information from visitors unless they choose to email us.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and operate, lease and maintain our properties.
  • To send operational text messages to partners who have opted in, as described in our Terms of Service & SMS Terms.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records and to comply with the law.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example, property managers, accountants, and our text-messaging provider, solely to deliver messages), and when required by law or to protect our rights.

Text messaging privacy

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text messaging opt-in data or consent with any third party, other than the text-messaging provider that delivers our messages on our behalf.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP or emailing tim.n@fournventures.com.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold, and we keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Opt-in records are kept for as long as you are enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards.

Children

This website and our text program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your contact information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing tim.n@fournventures.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
