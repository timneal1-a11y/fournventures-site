Privacy Policy

fourNventures, Ltd. ("we", "us") is a family-owned real estate company based in Texas, 6235 Retta Mansfield Rd, Burleson, TX 76028. This policy explains how we handle personal information.

Information we collect

We collect only the contact information needed to operate our business and to communicate with the people we work with, such as a name, email address or mobile phone number that a person gives us directly. This website does not use advertising trackers and does not collect personal information from visitors.

How we use it

We use contact information only to communicate about our business and, where someone has opted in, to send the text messages described in our SMS Terms.

Text messaging privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (for example, our text-messaging provider, solely to deliver messages) or when required by law.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.
